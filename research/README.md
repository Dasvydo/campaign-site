# Raw research

The four unedited research reports behind `ICP-RESEARCH.md`, kept because the campaign has
an evidence gate and every claim in the brief needs to be traceable to a URL.

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `raw-property.md` | Property administration. DK, LT, UK, US |
| `raw-accounting.md` | Accounting, bookkeeping and tax. DK, LT, UK, US |
| `raw-insurance.md` | Insurance broking. DK, LT, UK, US |
| `raw-competitive.md` | Competitors, Copilot, objections, go to market, DK/LT marketing law |

## How to read them

Each report labels its own claims. The labels differ slightly between reports because they
were written independently, but they all separate the same four things:

- **independent research with a disclosed method**, safe to cite
- **vendor published**, usable with attribution
- **unverified**, which must not be published
- **inference**, which is reasoning rather than fact

Each report ends with a list of what its author could not verify and why. **Read that list
before quoting anything from the body.**

## Known limits

- **Reddit was blocked** to the accounting, insurance and competitive research. Only the
  property research reached it, via RSS. That is why the property verbatims are much richer
  than the other two, and why the insurance practitioner vocabulary is under sampled.
- **G2, Gartner, AccountingWEB and Glassdoor** returned 403 throughout. Some AccountingWEB
  quotes are snippet sourced and are flagged as such.
- Several regulator PDFs would not extract, so some legal points rest on law firm
  summaries. Those are flagged, and `ICP-RESEARCH.md` section 14.2 lists what needs counsel.

These are research notes, not copy. Nothing here has been through the claims gate. The
distilled, gate checked version is `ICP-RESEARCH.md`.
